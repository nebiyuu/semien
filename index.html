Wildlife & nature

What we'll see on the mountain

The Simien Mountains are a UNESCO World Heritage site and home to some of Africa's rarest animals. I know exactly where to find them.

🦁
Gelada baboons
Herds of hundreds. We almost always find them on day one.
🐐
Walia ibex
Endangered mountain goats found only here. I know their spots.
🐺
Ethiopian wolf
One of the world's rarest canids. On longer treks, we go to where they roam.
🏔️
Dramatic escarpments
1,500m drops, jagged peaks, views that stop you mid-sentence.
Your guide
Ginbie Abebe
Licensed Simien Mountains Guide · Born & based in Debark

I was born and raised in Debark right at the gateway to the Simiens. I studied Geography at Gondar University, taught for three years, and I've spent the last 5 years taking trekkers through one of Africa's most dramatic landscapes. I know every trail, every endemic species, and every camp up here.

What people tell me afterward is that I kept them laughing, kept them learning, and kept them safe. When you trek with me, you're not booking a tour company. We're just two people and a great crew exploring a place I genuinely love.

When I'm not on the mountain, I'm a competitive runner I've won races across 12km and half-marathon distances in Gondar, Bahirdar, and Addis Ababa. I train right here in the Simiens. So yes, I can keep up.

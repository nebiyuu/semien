Wildlife & nature

What you'll see on the mountain

The Simien Mountains are a UNESCO World Heritage site and home to some of Africa's rarest animals.

🦁
Gelada baboons
Herds of hundreds. Almost guaranteed on every trek.
🐐
Walia ibex
Endangered mountain goats found only here.
🐺
Ethiopian wolf
One of the world's rarest canids. Spotted on longer treks.
🏔️
Dramatic escarpments
1500m drops, jagged peaks, and endless highland views.
